Reference architecture

One-Person AI Company

A local service business is not a funnel. It is a loop: marketing rings the phone, intake catches it, sales books it, operations does the job, finance gets you paid — and that money decides next week's ads.

The rule

AI moves information. You move money and risk.

Every step below is a human one. 5 are approval gates on money or a customer relationship; the rest is the work itself and the judgement immediately around it. None of them is here because it was hard to automate.

Reconcile quoted against actual and reprice

monthly

Checks what was quoted against what the job actually cost, and moves a price only when the evidence clears two gates: enough jobs of that type, and a drift interval that excludes zero. Underneath

Three plain files

Not a stage - the substrate under all five. Claude Code reaches the CRM, the job system and the books over MCP; underneath that sit three plain files the owner edits directly: what things cost, what the rules are, and who is on the bench. No agent works from memory.

pricing.yaml

What every line item costs. The quoting agent may read it; only the owner writes it.

rules.md

Service area, job types taken and refused, auto-send limit, payment terms, escalation thresholds. Written once, read by every agent.

bench.csv

Subs and crews in preference order, with trades and rates. This file is what makes a no-show recoverable in minutes.

Every threshold in this architecture is a number in one of these rather than a judgement an agent makes. That is what turns “is this quote too big to send unattended” from a prompt-engineering problem into a comparison.

Not built

The other 33

7 roles run the whole loop. The 33 below are what each would split into if volume ever justified it, and every one names the volume that would. They are backlog, all of them. A committed list of named agents that reads as built is how a customer-facing promise gets wired to something that is not there.

Intake agent

After-hours voice intake
Own the 6pm-8am calls with a different script and a next-morning callback promise. Splits out of the Intake agent; justified when after-hours volume passes 20 calls a month.
Daytime overflow voice intake
Pick up calls that arrive while another is live, instead of queueing them. Splits out of the Intake agent; justified when simultaneous-call rate passes 5% of volume.
SMS and text intake
Split texting off voice so tone and pacing can differ from a phone script. Splits out of the Intake agent; justified when text is over a third of inbound.
Web chat intake
Handle site visitors, who arrive with less context and need more qualifying. Splits out of the Intake agent; justified when the site produces 15+ chats a month.
Social DM intake
Instagram and Facebook messages, where identity and address are hardest to pin down. Splits out of the Intake agent; justified when social produces 10+ leads a month.

Qualifier agent

Service-type classifier
Map a described problem to a job type, which everything downstream keys off. Splits out of the Qualifier agent; justified when job types pass 12.
Service-area checker
Address-level geo and travel-time checks rather than a postcode list. Splits out of the Qualifier agent; justified when edge-of-area jobs start losing money on travel.
Urgency triage
Separate emergency from scheduled work and price the difference. Splits out of the Qualifier agent; justified when emergency work passes 15% of jobs.
Budget probe
Find the client's ceiling before a quote is built for a job they will never buy. Splits out of the Qualifier agent; justified when quote-to-win rate falls under 40%.
Spam and robocall filter
A dedicated screen once junk volume makes the intake agent's transcript costs material. Splits out of the Qualifier agent; justified when junk passes 25% of inbound.

Quoting agent

Scope extractor
Turn a rambling description and site photos into structured scope lines. Splits out of the Quoting agent; justified when scope errors cause 3+ change orders a month.
Material take-off
Expand scope into quantities and parts, which is a different skill from pricing them. Splits out of the Quoting agent; justified when material cost passes 40% of job cost.
Labour estimator
Estimate hours per job type from the closed-job record instead of a flat rate. Splits out of the Quoting agent; justified when labour variance passes 25% of estimate.
Quote follow-up chaser
Own the day-2 and day-5 chase and learn what wording gets replies. Splits out of the Quoting agent; justified when open quotes pass 30 at a time.
Upsell and bundle suggester
Propose the adjacent job while the crew is already on site. Splits out of the Quoting agent; justified when repeat-visit rate suggests bundling would land.

Dispatch agent

Crew assignment
Match trade, rating and history to the job rather than walking the bench in order. Splits out of the Dispatch agent; justified when the bench passes 10 names.
Bench fallback
Own the no-show recovery path, which is time-critical and needs its own escalation ladder. Splits out of the Dispatch agent; justified when no-shows pass 4 a month.
Route and travel-time optimiser
Sequence the day's jobs so travel is not silently eating the margin. Splits out of the Dispatch agent; justified when jobs per day passes 3.
Client ETA notifier
Live arrival windows, which is the single most-asked question in this trade. Splits out of the Dispatch agent; justified when 'where are they' calls pass 10 a month.
Weather and permit watcher
Catch the rained-off or unpermitted job before the crew is dispatched to it. Splits out of the Dispatch agent; justified when weather-cancelled jobs pass 2 a month.

Closeout agent

Pre-job checklist
Confirm access, parking, pets and power before the van leaves. Splits out of the Closeout agent; justified when aborted visits pass 2 a month.
Photo quality control
Reject a closeout pack whose photos will not survive a dispute. Splits out of the Closeout agent; justified when disputes pass 1 a month.
Change-order catcher
Own the scope diff and the on-site conversation prompt. Splits out of the Closeout agent; justified when unbilled changes pass $500 a month.
Warranty and callback logger
Track callbacks against the original job so repeat failures surface. Splits out of the Closeout agent; justified when callbacks pass 3 a month.

Collections agent

Payment reconciler
Match payments to invoices to jobs when partials and deposits make it non-obvious. Splits out of the Collections agent; justified when partial payments pass 10% of invoices.
Day-7 nudge writer
Own the warm reminder, tuned per client history. Splits out of the Collections agent; justified when overdue invoices pass 10 a month.
Day-30 demand writer
Own the firm message, which is a different register and higher stakes. Splits out of the Collections agent; justified when 30-day invoices pass 5 a month.
Deposit and retainer tracker
Track money taken before work, which ages differently from an invoice. Splits out of the Collections agent; justified when deposits are taken on more than a quarter of jobs.
Statement and receipt sender
Month-end statements for commercial clients who pay off statements, not invoices. Splits out of the Collections agent; justified when commercial clients pass 5.

Pricing analyst agent

Win-rate analyst
Separate lost-on-price from lost-on-timing, which the single agent conflates. Splits out of the Pricing analyst agent; justified when quote volume passes 100 a month.
Ad-channel allocator
Per-channel budget moves rather than one blended raise or cut. Splits out of the Pricing analyst agent; justified when channels pass 3.
Review requester
Ask for the review at the moment the job lands well, which is a narrow window. Splits out of the Pricing analyst agent; justified when review volume matters to local ranking.
Dormant-client reactivation
Work the back catalogue, which nothing else in the loop touches. Splits out of the Pricing analyst agent; justified when the client list passes 300.
What this page does not know

Every KPI on this map reads unmeasured, and the volumes are illustrative for a one-van shop. There is no current-state map behind it, so there is no hours-returned figure — ai_company.py hours refuses to produce one without a baseline. Tool costs are absent on purpose: phase 1 of the readiness framework fills them from real invoices, and a placeholder in a committed reference gets read as a fact.
