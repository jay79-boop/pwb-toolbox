One-Person AI Company - Local Service Business

A local service business is not a funnel. It is a loop: marketing rings the phone, intake catches it, sales books it, operations does the job, finance gets you paid — and that money decides next week's ads.

Where a person stays

Agents move information. A person moves money and risk. Every step below is a human one: 5 are approval gates on money or a customer relationship, and the rest is the work itself and the judgement immediately around it. None of them is here because it was hard to automate.

Underneath: plain files

Not a stage - the substrate under all five. Claude Code reaches the CRM, the job system and the books over MCP; underneath that sit three plain files the owner edits directly: what things cost, what the rules are, and who is on the bench. No agent works from memory.

pricing.yaml

What every line item costs. The quoting agent may read it; only the owner writes it.

rules.md

Service area, job types taken and refused, auto-send limit, payment terms, escalation thresholds. Written once, read by every agent.

bench.csv

Subs and crews in preference order, with trades and rates. This file is what makes a no-show recoverable in minutes.

What this page does not know
